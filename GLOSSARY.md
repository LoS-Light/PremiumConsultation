# Premium Consultation

The part of the GUM app where users meet the people who give one-to-one advice, and contact them by booking an appointment or sending a WhatsApp message.

## Language

**User**:
A person using the GUM app.
_Avoid_: Member, customer, client

**Specialist**:
A person on the ES team who gives users one-to-one consultations, shown on the Premium Consultation screen with a photo and a name.
_Avoid_: ES Specialist, GUM Specialist, 1-to-1 specialist, consultant, ES team member

**ES team**:
The GUM team of Specialists that receives appointment bookings and WhatsApp messages from users.
_Avoid_: GUM ES, consultation team
