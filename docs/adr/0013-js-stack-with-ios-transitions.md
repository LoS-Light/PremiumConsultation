# ADR-013: A JS stack with iOS transitions on both platforms

The author wants Android navigation to match iOS: the new screen slides in from the right over the previous one, which shifts a little to the left, leaves the same way, and a swipe from the left edge follows the finger back. The native stack (`@react-navigation/native-stack`, the original choice in SPEC §3) can't do this on Android here: its `ios_from_right` animation pushes correctly, but on pop the leaving screen goes blank and a white panel slides out (Android 15 emulator in Expo Go, even with a plain test screen), and it has no interactive swipe back on Android. So we use the JS stack (`@react-navigation/stack`) with `TransitionPresets.SlideFromRightIOS` and `gestureEnabled` on both platforms, at the cost of transitions drawn by React Native rather than the system (not noticeable with two screens), a close imitation of iOS's own push, and vertical-only drags on the content sheet and the bottom section so a horizontal swipe from the edge reaches the stack.
